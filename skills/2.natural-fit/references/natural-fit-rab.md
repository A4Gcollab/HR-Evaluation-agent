# Natural Fit — Research & Analysis Bureau (RAB) Role

> Source: docs/hr-evaluation-documentation.md Section 5F
> Status: ✅ Production-ready (validated against Suhani's evaluation reports)

---

## Trait 1 — Investigative Curiosity

**Definition:** Driven to dig deeper, verify information, and explore topics beyond
surface-level understanding. Does not accept information at face value without
questioning or cross-referencing.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Actively seeks multiple sources before forming conclusions
- Demonstrates independent exploration beyond what was assigned
- Asks probing questions that reveal depth of thinking
- Has examples of discovering something by digging deeper than expected

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Shows awareness of verification but limited depth in independent exploration
- Can describe checking facts but without proactive investigation
- Some curiosity present but primarily within assigned scope

**Score 1 indicators:**
- Accepts information at face value without questioning
- No evidence of independent exploration or verification
- Purely passive approach to information gathering

---

## Trait 2 — Critical Reading & Reasoning

**Definition:** Can analyze written material, identify bias, compare viewpoints,
and draw reasoned conclusions. Evaluates arguments for logical consistency
and evidentiary support rather than accepting claims uncritically.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Identifies bias and compares viewpoints with depth and nuance
- Can critique an argument by pointing out logical gaps or missing evidence
- Demonstrates evaluation of sources for credibility and relevance
- Shows evidence of changing a conclusion based on better reasoning

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Can identify bias and compare viewpoints; depth of critique still developing
- Shows awareness of different perspectives without deep analysis
- Some critical thinking present but not consistently applied

**Score 1 indicators:**
- Accepts arguments without evaluating logic or evidence
- Cannot identify bias or distinguish between opinion and fact
- No evidence of critical evaluation in any context

---

## Trait 3 — Accuracy Orientation

**Definition:** Prioritizes correctness over speed. Careful and responsible approach
to reporting, documentation, and data handling. Double-checks work before
considering it complete.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Has a systematic approach to verification (checklists, cross-references)
- Catches errors that others miss — specific examples of quality improvement
- Prioritizes correctness over speed explicitly in their approach
- Demonstrates responsible approach to reporting and documentation

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Prioritizes correctness over speed; responsible approach to reporting
- Shows care for accuracy but without systematic verification methods
- Some evidence of double-checking without demonstrated rigor

**Score 1 indicators:**
- Rushes through work without checking for accuracy
- No evidence of quality control or verification habits
- Errors in work are accepted rather than caught and corrected

---

## Trait 4 — Empathic Sense-Making

**Definition:** Understands multiple perspectives when analyzing situations or
people. Can put themselves in the shoes of different stakeholders to understand
motivations, constraints, and viewpoints. Maintains neutrality in analysis.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Demonstrates understanding of multiple stakeholder perspectives
- Maintains full neutrality when analyzing conflicting viewpoints
- Can articulate why different people hold different positions
- Balances empathy with objectivity in analysis

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Understands multiple perspectives but may not always maintain full neutrality
- Shows awareness of different viewpoints without deep perspective-taking
- Some empathy present but occasionally defaults to own viewpoint

**Score 1 indicators:**
- Analyzes from a single perspective without considering others
- Cannot articulate why someone might hold a different position
- No evidence of empathic reasoning in any example

---

## Trait 5 — Attention to Detail

**Definition:** Ensures thoroughness in documentation, data, and deliverables.
Uses systematic approaches (checklists, templates, review processes) to ensure
nothing is missed. Catches inconsistencies and gaps.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Has a checklist-based or template-based approach for ensuring quality
- Catches inconsistencies that others miss — specific examples
- Documents work thoroughly with proper structure and formatting
- Demonstrates systematic approach to ensuring document quality and accuracy

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Strong checklist-based approach for ensuring document quality
- Shows attention to detail but without systematic methods
- Some thoroughness present but occasionally misses things

**Score 1 indicators:**
- Submits work with frequent errors or inconsistencies
- No evidence of systematic quality checking
- Documentation is incomplete or poorly structured
