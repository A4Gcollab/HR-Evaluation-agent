# Natural Fit Subskill — Community Building Role

> Source: docs/hr-evaluation-documentation.md Section 5C

---

## Trait 1 — Relationship Building

**Definition:** Ability to form genuine, trust-based connections with community members
across different backgrounds and contexts.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Examples of building a network, group, or following from scratch
- Describes relationships with specificity — not just "I'm a people person"
- Follows up and maintains relationships over time
- Resume: student clubs, volunteer coordination, peer mentorship

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Has some relationship-building examples but without depth or specificity
- Demonstrates friendliness without evidence of sustained relationship effort
- Can describe connections but they appear transactional or role-based

**Score 1 indicators:**
- Claims to be good with people but no concrete example
- Relationships are transactional — formed for a purpose, not maintained
- No evidence of building community connections in any context

---

## Trait 2 — Event and Program Facilitation

**Definition:** Can plan, organize, and run community events or programs with minimal
supervision.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Has organized an event or program — describes logistics, challenges, outcomes
- Handles last-minute changes without losing focus
- Gathers feedback after events and improves next time
- Demonstrates end-to-end ownership of an event or program

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Has been involved in events but not as primary organizer
- Can describe event logistics without demonstrating challenge navigation
- Shows organizational ability without event-specific evidence

**Score 1 indicators:**
- Only attended events, never organized one
- Cannot describe a single logistical challenge they navigated
- No evidence of planning or facilitating any group activity

---

## Trait 3 — Empathy and Inclusion

**Definition:** Makes all community members feel welcomed, heard, and included
regardless of background, confidence level, or participation style.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Notices and reaches out to people who are quiet or disengaged
- Has handled a situation where someone felt excluded or uncomfortable
- Designs programs with diverse participation in mind
- Actively creates space for underrepresented voices

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Shows general awareness of inclusion without specific proactive examples
- Demonstrates empathy but in reactive rather than proactive ways
- Acknowledges diversity without evidence of active inclusion efforts

**Score 1 indicators:**
- Focuses only on engaged, vocal community members
- No example of proactively including someone on the margins
- No evidence of empathy or inclusion thinking in any context

---

## Trait 4 — Communication and Presence

**Definition:** Commands attention, energizes a room or group, and communicates with
warmth and clarity in both one-on-one and group settings.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Comfortable speaking to a group — not just comfortable in conversation
- Uses clear, accessible language when addressing diverse audiences
- Written communication (posts, messages, emails) is warm and engaging
- Demonstrates ability to energize or facilitate group interaction

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Clear communicator in one-on-one but limited group evidence
- Adequate presence without standout warmth or energy
- Can communicate but does not naturally command group attention

**Score 1 indicators:**
- Only comfortable in one-on-one settings
- Communication style is formal or stiff in group contexts
- No evidence of group-facing communication ability

---

## Trait 5 — Mission Connection

**Definition:** Deeply connected to the cause or mission behind the community.
Motivated by impact, not just activity.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Can articulate why this community's mission matters to them personally
- Has been part of a mission-driven group or initiative before
- Talks about community impact, not just community size
- Connects their motivation to Omysha Foundation's specific purpose

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Shows interest in the mission without personal connection depth
- Describes mission-driven work without articulating why it matters to them
- Some alignment with purpose but primarily activity-motivated

**Score 1 indicators:**
- Drawn to community building as an activity without connection to purpose
- Cannot explain what success would look like for this community
- No evidence of mission-driven motivation in any context
