# Natural Fit Subskill — Marketing Role

> Source: docs/hr-evaluation-documentation.md Section 5B

---

## Trait 1 — Creativity and Storytelling

**Definition:** Ability to generate original ideas, craft compelling narratives, and
communicate the brand's message in ways that resonate with the target audience.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Gives specific examples of creating content, campaigns, or messaging from scratch
- Explains a complex idea simply and engagingly during the interview
- Portfolio evidence or detailed past campaign descriptions
- Enthusiasm for visual, written, or digital content creation
- Shows range across content types or platforms

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Describes involvement in content creation but without originality signals
- Can articulate what good marketing looks like but limited evidence of producing it
- Some creative thinking present but not demonstrated with depth

**Score 1 indicators:**
- No original output to show or describe
- Can only explain what marketing is, not what they have made
- No evidence of creative initiative in any past experience

---

## Trait 2 — Audience Awareness

**Definition:** Understanding of who the target audience is and how to adapt tone,
platform, and message accordingly.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Adapts communication style based on platform (Instagram vs. LinkedIn vs. email)
- Can describe a specific audience segment and what matters to them
- Evidence of research or data use to understand audience behavior
- Demonstrates audience thinking when discussing Omysha Foundation's work

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Shows general awareness that different audiences exist
- Can identify an audience but without specificity about what drives them
- Some platform awareness without demonstrated adaptation

**Score 1 indicators:**
- One-size-fits-all thinking about content
- Cannot articulate who the audience is for a campaign they describe
- No evidence of considering the audience in any past work

---

## Trait 3 — Curiosity and Trend Awareness

**Definition:** Stays current with trends, platforms, and tools. Brings new ideas
proactively.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Mentions specific trends, tools, or platform updates unprompted
- Has experimented with new formats (Reels, newsletters, AI tools)
- Asks questions about the organization's current marketing approach
- Demonstrates a learning habit around marketing developments

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Aware of some current trends but without experimentation evidence
- Follows marketing content but does not demonstrate active exploration
- Shows interest in learning but limited proactive trend-seeking

**Score 1 indicators:**
- References only established platforms with no curiosity about emerging ones
- Cannot name a recent trend or tool they find interesting
- No evidence of staying current in the field

---

## Trait 4 — Execution and Consistency

**Definition:** Follows through on content plans, meets deadlines, and maintains brand
voice consistently across output.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Examples of managing a content calendar or campaign timeline
- Evidence of consistent output over a period (regular posting, newsletter series)
- Handles feedback on content without losing momentum
- Demonstrates reliability in delivery with specific examples

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Has completed marketing projects but without evidence of sustained consistency
- Describes meeting deadlines without demonstrating systematic execution
- Some follow-through evidence without a track record of consistency

**Score 1 indicators:**
- One-off projects only, no evidence of sustained execution
- Describes good ideas but cannot show follow-through
- No evidence of managing a content pipeline or meeting recurring deadlines

---

## Trait 5 — Data and Feedback Orientation

**Definition:** Uses performance data, engagement metrics, or audience feedback to
improve work.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- References specific metrics: reach, engagement rate, open rate, conversion
- Adjusted strategy or content based on what was or wasn't working
- Comfortable with testing and iterating — describes A/B testing or experimentation
- Uses data to support creative decisions, not replace them

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Mentions data awareness without specific examples of using it
- Acknowledges the importance of metrics without demonstrating application
- Open to feedback but does not proactively seek performance data

**Score 1 indicators:**
- Creates content purely on intuition with no mention of results or feedback
- Defensive when asked what they would do differently
- No evidence of measuring or learning from past content performance
