# Natural Fit Subskill — General (All Roles)

> Source: docs/hr-evaluation-documentation.md Section 4
> Used when no role-specific subskill exists or role is unspecified.
> Note in the evaluation report that general traits were applied.

---

## Trait 1 — Ownership

**Definition:** Takes personal responsibility for tasks and outcomes. Drives work
forward without needing constant direction. Does not wait to be told what to do next.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Examples of starting something without being asked
- Language showing agency: "I decided to", "I took it upon myself"
- Comfort with ambiguity — figures things out independently
- Resume evidence of leading or owning a project end-to-end

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Shows some responsibility but within structured, directed environments
- Can describe completing tasks independently but not initiating them
- Some ownership language without strong proactive evidence

**Score 1 indicators:**
- Lists responsibilities without showing personal agency
- Claims ownership but only within tightly managed environments
- No evidence of self-directed work in any context

---

## Trait 2 — Proactiveness

**Definition:** Anticipates needs, acts before being prompted, thinks ahead. Identifies
problems or opportunities before they become urgent.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Examples of doing more than what was asked
- Evidence of preparing or planning ahead without instruction
- Noticed a gap and acted on it without being told
- Came to interview prepared with specific knowledge about Omysha Foundation

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Shows willingness to do more when asked but limited self-initiation
- Some preparation evident but not consistently proactive
- Aware of gaps but waits for direction before acting

**Score 1 indicators:**
- All answers are reactive — no example of anticipating anything
- No evidence of going beyond the minimum in any past experience
- No preparation for the interview evident

---

## Trait 3 — Problem Solving

**Definition:** Breaks down challenges, identifies root cause, finds workable solutions.
Does not freeze or escalate every obstacle immediately.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Specific examples of solving a real problem from transcript or resume
- Logical and structured thinking when describing how they handled a situation
- Handles ambiguity without clear instructions
- Can think through a hypothetical during the interview

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Can describe a problem situation but without structured resolution approach
- Shows awareness of problem-solving without strong analytical evidence
- Some evidence of finding solutions but with significant support from others

**Score 1 indicators:**
- Only theoretical answers with no actual problem-solving example
- Every challenge example ends with escalating to a manager
- No evidence of independent problem resolution in any context

---

## Trait 4 — Communication

**Definition:** Expresses ideas clearly, structures thoughts coherently, adapts
communication to context. Listens actively and responds to what is actually asked.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Structured, coherent answers in transcript
- Responds directly to what was asked — not a rehearsed pivot
- Explains something complex simply when prompted
- Builds on interviewer's question, acknowledges nuance

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Generally clear communication but occasionally unfocused
- Answers questions adequately but without standout structure
- Some active listening evident but not consistently demonstrated

**Score 1 indicators:**
- Persistent difficulty maintaining a clear thread across the interview
- Answers are off-topic, circular, or never reach a point
- Over-rehearsed responses that ignore the actual question

---

## Trait 5 — Execution Ability

**Definition:** Follows through and delivers results. Manages time, meets commitments,
completes tasks to a satisfactory standard without constant supervision.

**Score 5 indicators:**
- Examples of completing projects or deliverables on time
- Evidence of managing multiple responsibilities without dropping things
- Consistency between commitments made and outcomes delivered
- Commits to 6-month internship with confidence

**Score 3 indicators:**
- Some completion evidence but without demonstrating time management
- Can describe finishing tasks but without evidence of managing competing priorities
- Shows commitment intention but with hedged language

**Score 1 indicators:**
- Multiple incomplete past engagements with vague explanations
- Commitment language heavily hedged: "I'll try", "depends on the situation"
- Resume shows frequent early exits from roles or projects
